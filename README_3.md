## Featured: Healthcare Fraud in America, FCA Settlement Analysis Series

> **Enforcement context, June 23, 2026:** DOJ's 2026 National Health Care Fraud Takedown (455 defendants, $6.5B+ alleged fraud) identified amniotic wound allograft billing as a primary fraud vector, charging 11 defendants in that category across six districts. CMS separately cut allograft reimbursement to $127/sq cm effective January 1, 2026. **This series flagged the same billing pattern in 145 providers using public CMS data and clinical plausibility thresholds, and its top-ranked outliers align with providers charged in the 2024-2025 allograft prosecutions.**

| Phase | Dataset | Key Finding |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| [Phase 1](https://github.com/IMHIRS-Analytics/healthcare-fraud-fca-analysis) | CMS T-MSIS Medicaid (10GB) | 2 home health agencies, mathematically impossible LPN billing, ~$123M exposure. OIG referral submitted. |
| [Phase 2](https://github.com/IMHIRS-Analytics/allograft_fraud) | CMS Medicare Physician (1.26M records, 9.66M rows) | 145 providers flagged nationally billing 50+ allograft units/patient against a clinical norm of 0-2. $758M in Medicare payments. NPs 55% of cohort, PTs 23%. Clusters: Arizona NP, SoCal PT (shared distributor pricing), Richardson TX, Colorado. |
| Phase 3 | DOJ/PACER cross-reference | Retrospective validation against federal enforcement outcomes. Of the screen's top 15 national outliers, 7 have been charged or pleaded guilty in allograft prosecutions (2024-2025); 5 ranked in the top 7 positions. |
| Phase 4 | HHS OIG LEIE (83,001 records) | Mississippi: 26.59 patient abuse exclusions per 100K, 2.6x the #2 state. Concentrated in ICF/IID facilities. |
